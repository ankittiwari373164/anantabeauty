Our story

Herbal care, with the receipts

We are a small herbal hair care brand from Bengaluru. This page is the long version of what we make, why we make it that way, and what we still need to show you.

Why plants, and why now

Rosemary, amla, brahmi, red onion and black seed have been part of hair care in many homes for a long time. The trouble is that a lot of modern products take a plant's name and bury it under a list of harsher ingredients, then leave you to guess what is doing the work.

We started from the other direction. Pick the plant actives first, write down what each one is in the bottle for, and only then decide what else is needed to make the formula stable and pleasant to use. That is why our product pages name the ingredients, and why the descriptions stay close to what the formula is meant to do.

The range, and what each part is for

Rosemary water sprays are the light daily step. They are meant for the scalp, on the days between washes, and they come in single and multi-pack sizes.

Onion and keratin shampoos are for cleaning the scalp properly. The keratin, red onion and black seed blend is built around the idea that a clean, balanced scalp is where stronger-feeling hair starts.

Anti-hair-loss serums are the treatment step. They use a blend of Redensyl, Anagain, Procapil and Follicusan, and come in 30 ml, 60 ml and 90 ml packs.

Combos bring a few of these together so you can start a fuller routine without picking each bottle yourself.

What we will not do

We will not invent reviews, invent before-and-after results, or make medical promises about hair regrowth.
