FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Shipping, payments, ingredients and what to do next.

How much is shipping?

Shipping is a flat ₹49, and orders of ₹499 or more ship free.

What is the return policy?

Read our returns policy and refund policy for the full details, or write to us with your order number.

How do I pay?

Choose pay on delivery at checkout and pay the courier in cash or UPI when your order arrives.

How do I track my order?

Sign in and open My account, then Orders. Each order shows its stage: placed, packed, shipped or delivered. Cancelled orders are shown as cancelled.

What is in the products?

Each product page lists its description, which names the main actives. The pack label carries the full ingredient list, and that label is the one to trust.

Are the products natural?

Our site describes the range as natural and Ayurvedic-inspired, and says the formulas are free from parabens, sulphates, alcohol and mineral oils. Read the ingredient list on your pack to see exactly what is inside.

Who can use these products?

Our product pages say they are suitable for men and women and for all hair types. If your scalp is sensitive, do a small patch test first.

Can these treat a medical condition?

No, and we do not claim they do. If you have a scalp condition, sudden hair loss, or you take medication that might affect your hair, please check with a doctor before you start.

Which product should I start with?

Start with the problem you notice most. Hair fall and thinning point to the serums. A dry or itchy scalp points to the shampoos. For a light daily step, the rosemary spray is the easiest. If you cannot decide, send us a message from the contact page.

Can I cancel an order?

Orders can be cancelled while they are still placed or packed. Ask us through the contact page with your order number. Once an order has shipped, cancellation is handled as a return.

Still stuck? Write to us.
